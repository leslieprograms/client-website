# Business Website

**SkinEstheticsLE.com** is a small local business that provides skin and cosmetic services. The site showcases a section for procedures for potential clients as well as a contact section where correspondence will be sent to the business owner's email account. Users are also able to view a demonstration video of services provided to previous clients.


## Features

- [x] Slideshow of cosmetic services that fade in and out sequentially
- [x] Columned lists of sevices provided
- [x] Business promotional video 
- [x] Biography of business owner
- [x] Contact form that visitors can utilize to send queries regarding appointments/services via the business owner's email
- [x] Google Maps for location accuracy
